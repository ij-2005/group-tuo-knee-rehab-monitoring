Frequently asked questions

Straightforward answers to common concerns.

Is my video stored or uploaded?

No. All processing happens in your browser using your device's own hardware. No video, image, or landmark data is ever uploaded to a server.

Do I need special equipment?

Just a webcam and a modern browser — Chrome or Edge recommended. No wearables, motion-capture suits, or special cameras required.

Is this a replacement for physical therapy?

No. This is a practice and monitoring tool, not a medical device. Always follow the guidance of a qualified healthcare professional.

What exercises does it support?

Currently three: squats, sit-to-stand, and knee flexion. More exercises are planned for future versions.

Why does it need to see my legs?

The system detects your hip, knee, and ankle positions to calculate knee joint angles. Your lower body must be fully visible in frame for accurate readings.

Does it work on a phone?

The interface works on mobile, but for best results we recommend a laptop or desktop with a front-facing webcam — a stable device improves pose detection accuracy.
